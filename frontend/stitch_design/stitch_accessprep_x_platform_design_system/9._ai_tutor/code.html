In simple interest, the principal stays unchanged every year. For example, on 1000 rupees at 10%, you earn 100 rupees in year 1 and 100 rupees in year 2. Total interest is 200 rupees.

In compound interest, interest earns interest. In year 1 you earn 100 rupees. For year 2, the new principal is 1100 rupees, so you earn 110 rupees. Total interest is 210 rupees. The difference of 10 rupees is interest calculated on the first year's interest.

Exam Shortcut: The difference for 2 years equals Principal times Rate squared divided by 10,000.
