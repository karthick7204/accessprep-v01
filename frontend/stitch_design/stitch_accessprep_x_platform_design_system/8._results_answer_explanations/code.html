menu_book Step-by-Step Grammatical Logic (The Proximity Rule)

Rule Definition: When two subjects are connected using correlative conjunctions such as ‘neither...nor’, ‘either...or’, or ‘not only...but also’, the verb must strictly agree with the subject closest to it. This is widely known in civil service examinations as the Rule of Proximity.

  1. First Subject: “the principal” (singular noun).
  2. Second Subject (Closest to Verb): “the teachers” (plural noun).
  3. Verb Selection: Because “the teachers” is plural and directly precedes the verb slot, the auxiliary verb must take the plural past form ‘were’ instead of the singular ‘was’.

Standard Exam Memory Trick: Ignore the first entity entirely when using ‘nor’. Read only: “...the teachers were present...” to verify agreement instantly.
